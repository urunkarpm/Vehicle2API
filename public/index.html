REST Endpoints Reference

GET /api/v1/trims
Fetch model trim variants with full specs (Engine, Transmission, Price, HP) filtered by model and country.
GET /api/v1/models
List vehicle models filtered by manufacturer slug, country market, or body type (SUV, Sedan, EV).
GET /api/v1/search
Global fuzzy search across manufacturers, vehicle models, and trims.
GET /api/v1/nhtsa/decode/:vin
Dynamic VIN decoder proxy bridge fetching realtime NHTSA vPIC specification data.
GET /api/v1/manufacturers
Retrieve global vehicle manufacturers filtered by market availability.
GET /api/v1/countries
List all supported regional market codes and details.
